TL;DR there is no single "best forex robot" — every top-10 list you've seen is an affiliate page ranking whoever pays most. What exists: a reliable filter. Demand a third-party verified live record (6+ months), honest maximum drawdown, an explained strategy with no martingale/grid, a hard stop-loss on every trade, and realistic claims. Why "best forex robot" rankings are broken

Search the phrase and you'll find dozens of near-identical "Top 10 Forex Robots 2026" pages. Understand what they are: affiliate revenue pages.
