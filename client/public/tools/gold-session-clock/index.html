Frequently asked questions

Which gold session is open right now?

The clock above shows it live in your local time; GMT reference is in the table.

When is the best time to trade gold?

The London–New York overlap (13:00–17:00 GMT) — peak liquidity, tightest spreads.

Is the gold market open 24 hours?

Nearly — Sunday evening to Friday evening, with a short daily rollover break where spreads widen.

Why does my broker's clock differ?

MetaTrader shows broker server time (often GMT+2/+3); this page uses your device's local time. DST also shifts boundaries an hour.
