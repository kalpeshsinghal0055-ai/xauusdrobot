Pip vs point on gold — the trap that confuses everyone

Forex pairs have a standard pip definition; gold doesn't quite. Most brokers treat a pip on XAUUSD as a $0.10 move and a point as $0.01. So when someone says their gold EA has a "500 point stop", that's a $5.00 move — the same as 50 pips. Always check which unit your platform or robot uses before comparing numbers.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a pip in gold?

Usually a $0.10 move — worth ~$0.10 at 0.01 lots and ~$10 at 1 lot.

Pip vs point?

Pip = $0.10, point = $0.01 → 1 pip = 10 points. Platforms often count gold in points.

How much is a $1 move worth?

10 pips / 100 points — about $1 at 0.01 lots, $100 at 1.00 lot.

Same on every broker?

The math holds wherever the 100 oz contract applies — check your broker's XAUUSD specification.
