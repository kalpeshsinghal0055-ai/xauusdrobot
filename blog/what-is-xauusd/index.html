What Is XAUUSD? The Symbol, the Quote and How Gold Actually Trades

By the XAUUSD Robot Team · Updated August 2026

Quick answer: XAUUSD is the price of one troy ounce of gold in US dollars. "XAU" is gold's international currency code (X = non-country asset, AU = aurum, Latin for gold); "USD" is the dollar. A quote of 2,412.50 means one ounce costs $2,412.50. Retail traders trade XAUUSD as a spot CFD on MT4/MT5 — profiting from price moves in either direction without owning metal — where 1 pip ≈ $0.10 and 1 lot = 100 oz.

The symbol, decoded

PartMeaning
XISO 4217 prefix for assets that belong to no country
AUFrom aurum — Latin for gold (the same AU as the periodic table)
USDThe quote currency — gold is priced in dollars worldwide

So XAUUSD reads exactly like a forex pair: how many of the second currency (dollars) buys one unit of the first (an ounce of gold). Silver follows the same pattern as XAGUSD (argentum). That's why gold lives on forex platforms and behaves, mechanically, like the world's most-traded "currency pair."

Reading the quote

XAUUSD = 2,412.50 means one troy ounce of gold costs $2,412.50 right now. A troy ounce is 31.1035 grams — the precious-metals standard, slightly heavier than a kitchen ounce. Like any traded instrument there are two prices: the bid (sell) and ask (buy), and the gap between them — the spread — is your cost per trade.

How XAUUSD is actually traded

  • Spot CFD (the retail standard): on MT4/MT5 through a broker — both directions, small sizes from 0.01 lots, leverage available. This is what "trading XAUUSD" almost always means. The full starting path: how to trade gold.
  • Futures (COMEX): institutional standard, large contracts, where much of the world's gold price is actually discovered.
  • ETFs: ownership-style exposure for investors — no leverage, no shorting ease, a different game entirely (investing vs trading).

XAUUSD vs owning gold

Trading XAUUSD gives you the price, not the metal: no storage, both directions, leverage, and pennies-level entry sizes — plus leverage's risks and overnight swap costs. Physical gold gives ownership with premiums and storage, and only profits one way. Neither is "better"; they answer different goals.

Why XAUUSD moves

Gold's price responds to a handful of measurable macro forces — the US dollar (inverse), real interest rates (inverse), inflation expectations, safe-haven fear flows, and central-bank buying. Frequently asked questions

What does XAUUSD mean?

Spot gold in US dollars — XAU (aurum) vs USD. The quote is the dollar price of one troy ounce.

It's gold's dollar PRICE as a symbol — you trade the price, not the metal.

Is XAUUSD a forex pair?

A metal quoted like a forex pair — trades on the same platforms with more volatility.

How much is 1 pip on XAUUSD?

$0.10 of price movement — worth ~$0.10 at 0.01 lots, ~$10 at 1 lot.

What hours does XAUUSD trade?

24/5 — Sunday evening to Friday evening, deepest in London–NY hours.
