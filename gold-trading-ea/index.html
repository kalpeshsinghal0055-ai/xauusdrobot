How the strategy works

The EA runs a trend-following, rule-based system on gold:

  1. Trend identification — it evaluates XAUUSD price structure to determine the dominant direction, rather than counter-trading spikes.
  2. Rule-based entries — positions open only when the strategy's conditions align; there is no discretionary or random element.
  3. Managed exits — every position carries a preset stop-loss and profit target, managed automatically to the close.
  4. News avoidance — around scheduled high-impact releases the EA stands aside, avoiding the worst spread and slippage conditions gold produces.

For context on how this compares with scalping, grid and martingale approaches, see gold EA strategy types explained. If you want a faster-cycle alternative, we also provide a separate XAUUSD scalping EA.

Risk management — the part that matters most

Our position is simple: most gold EAs fail not because their entries are bad, but because their risk model is reckless (martingale, no stop-loss, oversized lots). This EA is built the opposite way — capped risk per trade, hard stops, and no recovery mechanics. It will have losing trades; it is designed so no single trade or losing streak can wipe the account by itself.

  • Fixed-fractional sizing — risk scales with your balance, not with recent losses.
  • Hard stop-loss on every position — held at the broker, not just in the EA.
  • Drawdown-conscious behaviour — conservative defaults; you control the risk setting.
